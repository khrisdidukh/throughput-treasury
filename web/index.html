This treasury pays only for work at the constraint.

An autonomous business has money and vendors pitching it AI agents, contractors and tools. Most of those purchases make one step faster and the business no faster at all. Here, a private AI CFO judges each offer against the business's real flow, signs its answer inside a secure enclave, and a NEAR contract pays only if the signature checks out and the money goes to the bottleneck.

    How a payment is decided

    Three layers, and each one can say no. None of them can say yes alone.

    The engine finds the constraint

    A Theory of Constraints engine reads the workflow history and prices every open offer: real value (more delivered per week) versus paper value (hours freed where nothing was waiting). The snapshot's hash is committed on-chain.

    The CFO judges, inside a TEE

    The model runs in an Intel TDX enclave on NEAR AI Cloud and signs its verdict with a key that never leaves it. Offer text is data, never an instruction: the contract fixes the prompt byte for byte.

    The contract enforces and pays

    It checks the signature and the exact snapshot, then applies hard rules the AI cannot override: the offer must target the constraint, return at least 3× its price, and fit the weekly cap.

    Every decision, on-chain

    Read live from the contract. Check any proof in your browser: it re-hashes the stored bytes and verifies the enclave's signature without trusting this website.
